## Data

- [JPL Horizons](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/horizons/): Voyager 1 positions and distances.
- [JPL approximate planet positions](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/approx_pos.html) (Standish and Williams).
- [JPL planetary satellite mean elements](https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/sats/elem/).
- [NASA NSSDCA planetary fact sheets](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/).
- Astronomical Almanac low-precision Moon series. IAU WGCCRE 2015 spin axes.
- Galaxy and heliosphere measurements: GRAVITY 2021, Reid and Brunthaler 2020, Schönrich et al. 2010, Bennett and Bovy 2019, McConnachie 2012, Pietrzyński et al. 2019, Graczyk et al. 2020, Li et al. 2021, Mei et al. 2007, Blakeslee et al. 2009, Tully et al. 2014, Swaczyna et al. 2022.

## Images

Listed as they were sourced for the original app. Each file must be checked against this list when the images are added.

- Other planet and moon maps and the Tycho star map: [NASA 3D Resources](https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources).
- Cosmic Cliffs, Southern Ring Nebula, Tarantula Nebula, Stephan's Quintet, Cartwheel Galaxy and Webb's First Deep Field: NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI.
